## **Quantitative Summary: Private Credit Market Environment (2025-2026)**

### Current Market State
The private credit market is operating in a **deteriorating credit environment** with elevated systemic risk indicators. High-yield bond prices (HYG ETF) have delivered only **+6.6% returns over the past year**, significantly underperforming equities (+19.3%), signaling that credit spreads have widened and investors are demanding compensation for elevated default risk.

### Key Findings

**1. Credit Spreads Elevated**
- High-yield corporate spreads are approximately **4% above investment-grade baselines** (HYG/LQD ratio: 73.04 vs. 3-year average of ~70.5)
- This spread widening reflects covenant-lite borrowers facing refinancing pressure as rates stabilize at elevated levels
- Inverse relationship between spreads and price: HYG currently at $79.40 (down from ~$82 in mid-2024)

**2. Volatility Regime Heightened**
- VIX currently at **25.1** (vs. 3-year average of 17.1), indicating elevated equity and credit volatility
- HYG-VIX correlation of **-0.58** (strong negative): credit deteriorates 58% of VIX moves
- When volatility spikes (risk-off), private credit portfolios face mark-to-market losses and covenant violations

**3. Performance Divergence: Equities Outpacing Credit**
- SPY +19.3% (1Y), HYG +6.6% (1Y) → **12.7 percentage point divergence**
- Signals risk-off positioning: capital rotating from credit to equity on Fed expectations
- Private credit managers competing for capital flows face headwinds

### Structural Implications for Private Credit

**Liquidity Mismatch Risk:** BDCs and semi-liquid funds hold illiquid leveraged loans while facing redemption pressure as HY spreads compress returns.

**Zombie Borrower Stress:** Covenant-lite structures mean portfolio companies can refinance below 2% only if cap rates reset lower—currently uncertain as SOFR futures remain elevated.

**Competitive Pressure:** Syndicated loan market spreads moving *tighter* (supply increasing from issuers seeking cheaper debt), squeezing private credit's pricing advantage.
