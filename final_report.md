Furthermore, recent evaluations have noted that BDCs and other private lenders face liquidity pressures due to the mismatch of holding illiquid loans while simultaneously having to manage redemption requests. This is exacerbated by covenant-lite structures that may expose the system to 'zombie borrower' scenarios, suggesting a liquidity crunch could be a ticking clock as firms arrive at their refinancing milestones.
